## From memory, not re-read
- **Every Layout:** named composition primitives (stack, cluster, grid) in `composition/layout.css`.
- **CUBE CSS:** composition, utility, block, exception, in cascade layers.
- **Refactoring UI:** fixed spacing and type scales. De-emphasise labels instead of emphasising values.
- **Practical Typography:** line length near 65 characters on reading text, real tabular numbers in counts.
- **GOV.UK Design System:** buttons name their action. Avoid disabled buttons and say what's wrong instead. Undoable
  actions need no confirmation step.
- **WCAG 2.2:** 4.5:1 text contrast, enforced by test. Focus always visible. Anything by pointer also works by keyboard.
